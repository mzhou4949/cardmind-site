CardMind AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

CardMind AI ("CardMind", "we") is a business-card scanner and personal CRM. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address and, if you provide it, your name. Used to create and secure your account. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier and email Apple shares (which may be a private relay address).
  • Contact data you add — names, titles, companies, emails, phone numbers, and notes for the people you save. This is your data; we store it so the app can show it back to you.
  • Card photos — pictures of business cards you scan. Stored privately so you can view the original card later.
  • Tasks and chat history — follow-up tasks you create and your conversation with the in-app assistant, so both persist between sessions.
  • Usage counters — how many scans and AI requests you use each month, to enforce plan limits.

We do not collect your device's contact list, location, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or ad trackers.

How AI processing works

When you scan a card, its photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to extract the contact details. When you use the assistant, your saved contacts and tasks are included so answers can be grounded in your own network, and — only when you ask it to look something up — the assistant may search the public web. Anthropic processes this data to provide the response; per Anthropic's API terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage). Every record is protected by row-level security: your data is readable and writable only by your signed-in account. Card photos live in a private bucket accessible only to you. Sessions on your device are encrypted, with keys held in the iOS Keychain.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your contacts with other users or third parties, except the AI processing described above.
  • We never send messages on your behalf to people you've saved without your explicit confirmation.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your account, all contacts, card photos, folders, tasks, chat history, and usage records. This is immediate and irreversible.

Crash reporting

If the app crashes, technical details about the error (not your contacts or photos) may be reported to Sentry to help us fix bugs.
