4. AI processing

In plain language: card photos and, when you use the assistant, your own contacts are sent to an AI model to do the work you asked for. That data is used to produce the answer and is not used to train the model.

CardMind uses Anthropic's Claude models via Anthropic's API for three functions:

  • Card extraction — the card photo(s) you capture are sent to the model, which returns structured contact fields and a short summary.
  • Assistant — when you send a message, your saved contacts and tasks are included as context so the assistant can answer about your network. It can also record information you explicitly ask it to save (for example, "add to notes that…") and display lists of your contacts.
  • Web research — only when you ask the assistant to look someone or something up, it may search the public web. Results are shown to you as suggestions with their sources; nothing is written to a contact unless you tap to save it.

Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, inputs and outputs sent through the API are not used to train Anthropic's models. Requests are processed on Anthropic's infrastructure and are subject to Anthropic's privacy practices. AI output can be wrong; you should review extracted details and suggested updates before relying on them.

7. Where data is stored and how it's protected

  • Access control — every record is protected by database row-level security tied to your account: your data can be read or changed only by requests authenticated as you.
  • Card photos — stored in a private bucket; the app accesses them through short-lived signed links.
  • In transit — all traffic between the app, our providers, and AI services is encrypted with TLS.
  • On your device — your sign-in session is encrypted, with the key held in the iOS Keychain.
  • Secrets — AI service credentials live only on our servers, never in the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal data we will notify you and any relevant authorities as required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists so the app can keep working for you. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), your account and all associated contacts, card photos, folders, tasks, conversation history, and usage records are removed immediately and permanently. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged on our providers' standard rotation, and server logs held by our infrastructure providers expire on their standard schedules. We may retain minimal records where the law requires it.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Much of this you can do directly in the app:

  • Access and correct — everything is visible and editable in the app.
  • Port — Settings → Export All Contacts produces a CSV; individual contacts export as vCards.
  • Delete — Settings → Delete Account, or delete individual contacts and photos at any time.
  • Permissions — camera, photo library, and notification access are controlled in iOS Settings.

For anything else, email mzhou6213@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days and may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder. If you are in the EEA or UK you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
